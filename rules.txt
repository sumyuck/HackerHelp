# HackerHelp Global AI Hackathon Rules & Guidelines

## Team Size Requirements
All teams must have a minimum of 3 members and a maximum of 5 members. Teams with fewer than 3 members at the time of submission will be disqualified.

## Submissions
Projects must be submitted before the official submission deadline. The submission must include:
- A GitHub repository link containing the code.
- A 3-minute video presentation/demo link.
- Documentation detailing the project name, problem statement, solution description, and tech stack.

## Rubrics & Scoring
Submissions will be scored on a scale of 1 to 10 by a panel of judges across the following criteria:
1. Innovation & Originality
2. Technical Complexity & Implementation
3. Scalability
4. UI/UX & Presentation
5. Business Value & Impact
