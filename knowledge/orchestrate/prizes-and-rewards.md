# Prizes and rewards (October 2026 edition)

## 1st place

- $1,200 cash prize
- Opportunities with leading tech companies
- 1:1 chat with industry experts
- HackerRank exclusive merchandise

## 2nd to 5th place

- $300 cash prize each
- Opportunities with leading tech companies
- HackerRank exclusive merchandise

## Every participant

- 10 HackerRank AI Mock Interview credits
- Certificate of participation

## How winners are contacted

Winners are contacted by the HackerRank team by email at their registered address after results are announced. Questions about the status of a specific prize payment, merchandise shipment, or interview opportunity are account-specific and must be handled by the HackerRank team. Open a support ticket or email help@hackerrank.com. Never post bank, payment, or address details in a public channel.
