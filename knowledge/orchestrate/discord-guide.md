# Orchestrate Discord channel guide

Use the right channel so your question reaches the right people.

## Welcome and info

- **#welcome**: start here when you join.
- **#about**: what Orchestrate is.
- **#rules-regulations**: server and event rules. Read these first.
- **#announcements**: official updates such as new editions, dates, and when feedback emails go out. You can follow this channel to get updates in your own server.

## Community space

- **#introduce-yourself**: say hello.
- **#general-chat**: general discussion with other participants.
- **#build-in-public**: share what you are building and get feedback.
- **#networking**: connect with other developers and recruiters.
- **#off-topic**: anything unrelated to Orchestrate.
- **#orchestrate-feedback** (forum): feedback about the event itself.

## Organizer space

- **#ask-the-team**: quick questions for the organizers. Search here and in the FAQ first, because many questions have already been answered.
- **#hacker-help-desk** (forum): one post per individual issue that needs the team, such as a submission problem, a missing email, or an interview issue. Include your edition, what you tried, and any error message. Do not post your email address, payment details, or other personal data publicly.
