# Frequently asked questions

## Is Orchestrate solo or team-based?

Solo. Every participant builds and submits their own agent, and you must be the author of your submission.

## Do I need prior AI experience?

HackerRank does not publish a formal prerequisite. You should be comfortable writing code and using AI coding tools. In the first edition, 44% of the top 50 were students, so early-career developers can compete well.

## Can I use my own tools and IDE?

Yes. Use whatever you normally work with, for example Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, GitHub Copilot, or any other assistant or editor.

## When do I get the problem statement?

The problem is emailed to your registered email address when the challenge starts. For October 2026 that is October 24, 2026 at 6:00 PM IST.

## How does the AI judge work?

After you submit, you take a 30-minute voice interview with an AI judge that has access to your submission. It asks about your architecture, decisions, tradeoffs, failure modes, and how you used AI while building.

## What do I need to submit?

Your code, your agent's output, and your AI chat transcript (`log.txt`).

## Who can participate?

The October 2026 event page does not list eligibility restrictions. Register on the event page. If you are unsure whether you are eligible, ask in #ask-the-team.

## Where do I get my feedback or results?

Results for October 2026 are announced on October 27, 2026. Feedback is emailed to your registered address. For the September 2026 edition, feedback emails went out on September 22, 2026. If you cannot find yours, check your spam folder first.

## I placed in the top 5. How do I claim my prize?

The HackerRank team contacts winners by email. Questions about a specific payment or shipment need a human. Open a support ticket or email help@hackerrank.com.

## How do I contact the HackerRank team?

Ask in #ask-the-team or open a ticket in #hacker-help-desk on Discord, or email help@hackerrank.com.
