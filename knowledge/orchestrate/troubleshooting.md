# Troubleshooting common issues

## I did not receive the problem statement email

1. Check your spam, promotions, and updates folders for mail from HackerRank.
2. Confirm you registered with the address you are checking.
3. Wait a few minutes after the official start time, because large email sends can take a while.
4. If it still has not arrived, open a support ticket. Do not post your email address in a public channel.

## My log.txt chat transcript was not created

- Make sure your AI coding tool actually read `AGENTS.md`. Some tools only read it when asked, so tell your assistant to read `AGENTS.md` before starting.
- Check the correct location: `$HOME/hackerrank_orchestrate/log.txt` on macOS/Linux, or `%USERPROFILE%\hackerrank_orchestrate\log.txt` on Windows.
- Do not hand-edit or reformat the log. In the first edition about half the submitted transcripts were unusable because the logging format was not preserved.

## My submission upload fails

- Make sure the code zip excludes `node_modules`, virtual environments, build output, data folders, and CSVs. These make the file much larger.
- Retry on a stable connection and a different browser.
- If it keeps failing near the deadline, take a screenshot of the error and open a support ticket right away.

## My AI judge interview will not start

- Allow camera and microphone access in your browser settings, and close other apps that use the camera.
- Use a recent version of Chrome or Edge on a stable connection.
- If the link has expired or you missed the window, open a support ticket. Rescheduling is decided by the HackerRank team case by case.

## I did not receive my feedback email

Check your spam folder first. Feedback goes to your registered email address. If it is still missing a few days after the announcement, open a support ticket.

## Something else

Ask in #ask-the-team, open a post in #hacker-help-desk, or email help@hackerrank.com.
