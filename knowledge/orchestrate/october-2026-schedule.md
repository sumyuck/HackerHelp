# October 2026 edition: schedule and registration

## Key dates (IST)

| Milestone | When |
| --- | --- |
| Challenge starts (problem statement emailed) | October 24, 2026, 6:00 PM IST |
| Build window | 24 hours from the start |
| AI judge interviews | Open immediately after you submit |
| Live evaluation | October 26-27, 2026 |
| Results announced | October 27, 2026 |

The October edition was announced in the Orchestrate Discord on September 28, 2026 with the message to save October 24 on your calendar.

## How to register

Register on the event page: https://www.hackerrank.com/hackerrank-orchestrate-october26 and click "Register Now". Use an email address you check regularly. The problem statement, submission instructions, and feedback are all sent to your registered email.

## Time zone tip

6:00 PM IST is 12:30 PM UTC on October 24, 2026. Convert this to your local time zone ahead of time so you do not miss the start.
