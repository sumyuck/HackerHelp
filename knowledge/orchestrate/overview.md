# What is HackerRank Orchestrate?

HackerRank Orchestrate is a 24-hour hackathon where each participant designs, builds, and ships an AI agent that solves a real-world problem. HackerRank describes it as competitive programming reinvented for the AI era: the developer acts as an orchestrator of AI agents and coding tools.

## Format

- Duration: 24 hours.
- Solo event: every participant builds and submits on their own.
- The problem statement is not published in advance. It is delivered by email when the challenge starts.
- Participants can use whatever tools they normally work with, for example Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, or any other AI coding assistant or IDE.
- After submitting, each participant completes a 30-minute voice interview with an AI judge.

## Goals of the event

Orchestrate has two stated goals: help the developer community become more fluent with AI, and connect AI-fluent developers with companies that want to hire them. Top finishers receive opportunities with leading tech companies in addition to cash prizes.

## Editions

Orchestrate runs as a recurring (roughly monthly) series. Each edition has its own event page and problem:

- May 2026 (first edition): held May 1-2, 2026. Participants built a terminal-based support triage agent.
- June 2026: second edition, held June 19, 2026.
- August 2026: challenge started August 1, 2026.
- September 2026: feedback emails for this edition were sent on September 22, 2026.
- October 2026: challenge starts October 24, 2026 at 6:00 PM IST. Registration: https://www.hackerrank.com/hackerrank-orchestrate-october26
